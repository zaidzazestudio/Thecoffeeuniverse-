Our Story

About

From Bergamo to Geneva. A story about lockdowns, lake shores, and the relentless pursuit of the perfect cup.

"
There is nothing worse than a messy counter — and nothing better than a coffee that makes you stop and think.

Universe Specialty Coffee was born from a decision made during the Covid lockdowns, when Maurizio Boi felt the walls of Bergamo caving in. He had spent years training at Bugan Coffee Lab, mastering espresso extraction, latte art, and the art of sourcing.

When the world opened again, Maurizio looked west. Geneva — wealthy, international, and underserved by specialty coffee — offered exactly the opportunity he was looking for.

The space is small. That's intentional. Universe is one barista, one counter, and one extraordinary conversation about where your coffee came from.

MB
Maurizio Boi
Owner & Head Barista

The Founder

From Bergamo with Love & Skills

Maurizio trained at Bugan Coffee Lab in Bergamo, where he developed his precision-first approach to espresso and his obsession with single-origin lots.

In 2025, he placed second at the Swiss Latte Art Championships — competing against the best baristas in one of the world's most coffee-serious countries.

At Universe, you'll find him behind the bar, pulling shots on the Modbar, and willing to talk at length about whatever is currently in the grinder.

🥈

2nd Place — Swiss Latte Art Championships 2025

Competing at national level among Switzerland's top specialty baristas.

How We Think

Our Philosophy

01

Small is Better

An intimate café allows for real conversation. We know your order. That is not something a large operation can offer.

02

Spotless Always

Nothing disrupts a great coffee more than a cluttered counter. We keep Universe immaculate — because cleanliness is part of the craft.

03

Single-Origin, Always

We do not blend. Every cup tells the story of one place. We want you to taste Nensebo — not a compromise.
